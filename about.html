About RepairConnect AI

Repair should be the easier choice.

RepairConnect AI helps people understand what's wrong with a damaged item, decide whether repairing is worthwhile, find the right repair professional, and track the repair — all in one place.

The problem

When something breaks, most people can't tell what's wrong, what it costs to fix, or whether repair is worth it — so they default to replacing, which is costlier and wasteful.

How AI helps

AI-assisted visual analysis identifies the item and visible damage, proposes possible causes, and suggests safe troubleshooting — always clearly preliminary, never overconfident about hidden damage.

Why repair matters

Repairing extends a product's life, keeps working devices out of the waste stream, and supports local businesses. RepairConnect AI makes the repair path the easy path.

How you benefit

A clear diagnosis, an honest repair-vs-replace decision, a smart match to nearby repair professionals, and transparent tracking — from "it's broken" to "it's fixed."
