Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about how RepairConnect AI works — and what is live versus demo today.

What is RepairConnect AI?

An AI-powered repair decision and connection platform. It helps you understand what's wrong with a damaged item, decide whether to repair or replace it, find nearby repair professionals, and track the repair.

How does AI analysis work?

You upload a photo (or describe the issue). The backend sends it to an AI provider (OpenAI, with Groq as fallback) through a secure function, and the response is normalized into a structured diagnosis. In the current build this is simulated with clearly-labelled demo data.

Can I upload an image?

Yes — JPG, PNG, and WebP up to 5 MB are accepted. The upload interface validates type and size before anything is sent.

Can I upload video?

Short videos are supported in the interface, but video analysis depends on the AI model's capabilities. Images are the reliable primary input.

Is the diagnosis guaranteed?

No. AI diagnoses are preliminary, based only on what is visible, and may be wrong. Always confirm with a professional before acting.

Should I always repair instead of replace?

No. RepairConnect gives you a transparent estimate so you can decide. Sometimes replacement genuinely is the more sensible choice.

How are repairers selected?

Repairers are ranked deterministically by distance, rating, price, expertise, and turnaround — the AI only explains the result. In the current build, providers are clearly-labelled demo data.

Is my uploaded media private?

Yes. Media is scoped to your account, and Firestore/storage rules prevent other users from accessing your files.

Does RepairConnect AI store my data?

Only what the flow needs: account details, devices, diagnoses, and repair requests. Data is isolated per user. See the Privacy Policy.

Is the AI currently live?

The AI backend is implemented in code but not active until the project's API keys are configured. Until then, results are clearly-labelled demo data.

Is the repairer data real or demo?

Demo. All providers shown are fictional samples, never presented as real businesses.

How does repair tracking work?

Every request has a status timeline (Requested → Accepted → Scheduled → Received → In Progress → Ready → Completed). Status changes are append-only history entries.
