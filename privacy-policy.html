Overview

RepairConnect AI is an AI-powered repair decision and connection platform. This Privacy Policy explains what information the application may collect, how it is used, and the choices you have.

RepairConnect AI is a hackathon project. The statements below distinguish between what is currently implemented and what is planned but not yet connected.

Implementation status

Currently implemented: the frontend runs in your browser. In demo mode it uses clearly-labelled sample data and stores small local preferences (such as your theme choice) in your browser's localStorage. No data is sent to a server.

Planned (not yet live): Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, AI providers (OpenAI primary, Groq backup), and media storage (Memcode primary, Firebase Storage backup). These are implemented in code but activate only once the project is configured and deployed.

Information we may collect

When the planned backend is live, the application may collect the information described below — always limited to what the repair flow needs.

  • Account information: name, email address, and (optionally) a profile photo.
  • Uploaded repair media: photos or videos of a damaged item you submit for analysis.
  • Device information: category, brand, model, and approximate age you provide.
  • Diagnosis information: the structured AI analysis result, including severity, confidence, possible causes, troubleshooting steps, and repair-vs-replace estimates.
  • Repair requests: the repairer you select, estimated cost, notes, and repair status history.
  • Location information: only an approximate location when you grant permission to find nearby repairers. Precise coordinates are not stored.

Usage information & local storage

The application uses localStorage for essential, non-personal preferences — for example your Light/Dark theme choice. See the Cookie Policy for details.

No third-party analytics or advertising trackers are used.

AI processing

Damage images and descriptions are sent to an AI provider (OpenAI, with Groq as fallback) through a secure server-side function. The AI returns structured results that are validated before display. AI output is treated as untrusted data and is never executed as code.

Third-party services

The planned architecture uses the following services: Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Functions), an AI provider (OpenAI primary / Groq backup), and media storage (Memcode primary / Firebase Storage backup). These providers process data only as necessary to deliver the service, under their respective terms and policies.

Data security

Private data is isolated per user and enforced by Firestore and storage security rules: a user can only access their own devices, diagnoses, uploads, and repair requests. API keys and secrets are kept server-side and never shipped to the browser. See the Security page.

Data retention

Data is retained only as long as needed to provide the service. Deleting a diagnosis or repair request removes its records and associated media references (and media where supported).

Your rights

You can update your profile, preferences, and saved devices, and delete your own data from the application. You may also contact us to request deletion of your information.
